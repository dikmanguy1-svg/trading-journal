Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

TradeInk ("we", "the service") is a personal trading journal. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights. Contact: dikmanguy1@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and a unique account ID.
  • Journal content: trades, watchlist, notes, daily answers, settings and progress (XP, badges) that you enter. This is stored in your private account on Google Firebase (Firestore, EU region). Other users cannot read it. The site operator can technically access stored data, and does so only to run the service, to help you when you ask, or when the law requires it.
  • Optional marketing consent: if you choose to join the waitlist or agree to updates, we store your email, your consent, the date and your language.
  • Device storage: your language choice and a few settings are saved in your browser (localStorage). We do not use advertising cookies or analytics.

Third parties

  • Google (Firebase Authentication and Firestore, Google Fonts): sign-in, storage and fonts. They receive ordinary web request data such as your IP address.
  • Finnhub: to show prices, the ticker symbols you enter and your IP address are sent to Finnhub.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website and may log visits.
  • AI coach (Cloudflare and Google Gemini): only when you send a question to the coach, your question, the trades and strategy shown in the coach window and any chart image you attach pass through our server on Cloudflare to Google Gemini to create the answer. Your name and email are not sent. Our server keeps only a daily usage count, not the content.

How we use it

To run the service, keep your journal, show prices and, only if you agreed, send updates. We do not sell your data.

Your rights

You can download your data at any time using "Download my data" when logged in. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your account and data, or withdraw marketing consent, by emailing dikmanguy1@gmail.com. Marketing emails will include an unsubscribe option.

Security and retention

Data is protected by access rules so each user can read only their own journal. No online service is perfectly secure. We keep your data until you ask us to delete it.

Children

People under 18 may use the service only with the knowledge and approval of a parent or legal guardian, who is responsible for their use. We do not knowingly collect marketing consent from minors. A parent or guardian may contact us to access or delete a minor's data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

TradeInk ("we", "the service") is a personal trading journal, run by an individual in Israel. This policy explains what we collect, why, who receives it and your rights. Contact: dikmanguy1@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and a unique account ID. When you create an account with email and password we receive your email address and a unique account ID. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores it only in a protected, scrambled (hashed) form; we never see or store your password. If you ask to reset your password, Firebase sends the reset email for us.
  • Journal content: trades, watchlist, long-term holdings, notes, screening and daily answers, your rules, settings and progress (XP, levels, perks) that you enter. This is stored in your private account on Google Firebase (Firestore, EU region). Other users cannot read it. The site operator can technically access stored data, and does so only to run the service, to help you when you ask, or when the law requires it.
  • Wick usage: to enforce daily limits, our server keeps a count of how many messages and images your account sent today, linked to your account ID. It never stores the content. The count is deleted automatically after 48 hours.
  • Optional marketing consent: if you choose to join the waitlist or agree to updates, we store your email, your consent, the date and your language.
  • Device storage: your browser (localStorage) keeps your language, theme and display choices, accessibility settings, whether you accepted Wick's notice and today's Wick count, a temporary copy of your journal from before you signed in, and the last known prices of your tickers so totals show right away. Firebase keeps your sign-in on this device (or only until you close the browser, if you untick "Remember me"). We do not use advertising cookies or analytics.

Wick, the AI coach

  • Optional, in testing: Wick is optional and the rest of the site works without it. It is a test feature open to signed-in users aged 18 and over. While we test, Wick is not offered to people located in the European Union, the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland.
  • What is sent: only when you send Wick a message: your message, the data you choose under "Wick sees" (for example trades, entry reasons, notes, strategy and rules), summaries calculated from it, and any chart image you attach. Your name and email are not sent. Our server checks your sign-in before it passes the request on.
  • Who receives it: the request passes through our server on Cloudflare to the AI provider. Today the provider is Google Gemini (free tier). Under Google's terms for this tier, Google may use what is sent and the answers to improve its products, and human reviewers may read them. Do not send anything sensitive. For this use Google decides on its own how it uses the data, under Google's terms and privacy policy. Before we market the service we plan to move to a paid provider that does not train on this data, and we will update this policy.
  • Our server: Cloudflare runs our Wick server on our behalf. It passes the request on and keeps only the daily count described above. It does not store your messages, images or answers.
  • Images: a screenshot may show an account number, a name or a balance. We do not remove these automatically. Crop them out before you upload.
  • History: the chat is kept only on your open page and is gone when you close it. We do not save it.
  • Your choice: you can stop using Wick at any time. Data already sent to Google cannot be recalled by us.

Third parties

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Google Fonts, Gemini): sign-in, storage, fonts and Wick's answers. They receive ordinary web request data such as your IP address.
  • Finnhub: to show prices, the ticker symbols in your journal and your IP address are sent to Finnhub.
  • Cloudflare: runs our Wick server. It may process technical data such as your IP address. We do not log message content or images.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website and may log visits.

Transfers outside Israel

These providers operate outside Israel, mainly in the United States and the European Union. Your journal is stored in the EU region. Data sent to prices, sign-in and Wick may be processed in the United States, where data protection may differ from Israel.

How we use it

To run the service, keep your journal, show prices, give Wick's answers and, only if you agreed, send updates. We do not sell your data and we do not show you ads.

Your rights

When logged in you can download your trades ("Download my data") or a full backup of your journal ("Download backup") at any time. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your account and data, or withdraw marketing consent, by emailing dikmanguy1@gmail.com. Marketing emails will include an unsubscribe option. You may also complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Security and retention

Data is protected by access rules so each user can read only their own journal. No online service is perfectly secure. We keep your journal until you ask us to delete it. Deleting your account deletes your journal and marketing consent.

Children

People under 18 may use the journal only with the knowledge and approval of a parent or legal guardian, who is responsible for their use. Wick is not available to people under 18. We do not knowingly collect marketing consent from minors. A parent or guardian may contact us to access or delete a minor's data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. If a change affects how we use data you already gave us, we will tell you on the site.

Firebase שומר את ההתחברות שלך במכשיר (או רק עד סגירת הדפדפן, אם ביטלת את "זכור אותי"). אין שימוש בעוגיות פרסום או באנליטיקה.

  • מי מקבל: הבקשה עוברת דרך השרת שלנו ב-Cloudflare אל ספק ה-AI. היום הספק הוא Google Gemini (התוכנית החינמית). לפי התנאים של Google לתוכנית הזו, Google רשאית להשתמש במה שנשלח ובתשובות לשיפור המוצרים שלה, ובודקים אנושיים עשויים לקרוא אותם. אל תשלח מידע רגיש. בשימוש הזה Google מחליטה בעצמה איך להשתמש במידע, לפי התנאים של Google ומדיניות הפרטיות שלה. לפני שנשווק את השירות, אנחנו מתכננים לעבור לספק בתשלום שלא מאמן על המידע הזה, ונעדכן את המדיניות.

Settings and backup
Your journal is saved to your account when logged in. Export a backup now and then.

TradeInk is a personal record-keeping tool, not investment advice. Prices come from Finnhub and may be delayed or unavailable. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell, and trading involves risk of loss. Prices are provided as-is with no guarantee of accuracy. When you log in, your journal, name and email are stored in your private account with Google Firebase and are not shared with anyone else; without logging in nothing is saved.
